Privacy Policy for SpringBoot Lab

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 | Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpringBoot Lab ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by the development team (Contact: sangitamanna272@gmail.com), collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Android mobile application.

We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. SpringBoot Lab is built primarily as an educational and offline-first developer tool.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • User-Generated Content: Custom code snippets, notes, project files created within the built-in sandbox IDE, and algorithmic problem submissions.
  • Community Forum Contributions: Questions, replies, and code snippets you choose to post in the collaborative Q&A debugging forum.
  • Display Profile Name: A chosen handle or username for peer recognition on the forum.

B. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

  • Learning Progress & Achievements: Completed Spring Boot lessons, code editor drafts, XP points, developer rank, and daily streaks.
  • Local Database: All progress, custom projects, and drafts are stored locally on your device using an offline Room Database.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Network Status: The app checks whether an active internet connection exists solely to pause or resume cloud synchronization and offline queues.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Basic, anonymized system diagnostic information (e.g., app version, device model, Android OS version) may be processed in the event of an unexpected crash to assist in debugging and stability improvements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Providing and personalizing the interactive Spring Boot learning curriculum.
  • Allowing you to write, save, compile, and run code snippets and sandbox projects.
  • Facilitating the peer-to-peer developer community forum.
  • Maintaining offline functionality and syncing your data when connectivity is available.
  • Improving application performance, stability, and fixing software bugs.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

3. Permissions Requested

SpringBoot Lab requests minimal, least-privilege permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to fetch forum discussions, sync pending offline updates, and access external developer documentation.

The app does not access your precise GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, or external storage files.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The App may utilize standard infrastructure services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services: For app delivery, security verification, and platform updates.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase: For secure backend database synchronization and system telemetry.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Storage: All your offline projects, code files, and progress remain on your device until you delete them within the app or clear the app's cache and data through Android System Settings.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You have the right to request deletion of any cloud-synced account data or forum posts associated with your profile. To request data deletion, contact us at: sangitamanna272@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

SpringBoot Lab is designed for students, software engineers, and general programming enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such data.

7. Security of Your Data

We employ industry-standard security practices to protect your data both locally and in transit. Network communications are secured using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) protocols.
